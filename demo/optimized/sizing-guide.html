Weighted Blanket Weight & Sizing Guide for Hot Sleepers

Sleep physicians and occupational therapists recommend choosing a weighted blanket that equals approximately 10% of the sleeper's body weight, plus 0.5 kg to 1.0 kg if the user prefers firmer deep touch pressure. For a 70 kg (154 lb) adult hot sleeper, the optimal weight is a 7.5 kg (16.5 lb) breathable cooling weighted blanket such as the 7.5 kg NordicSleep Organic Linen Weighted Blanket.

How Heavy Should a Cooling Weighted Blanket Be for a 70 kg Adult?

A 70 kg adult achieves balanced deep touch pressure stimulation with a 7.5 kg cooling weighted blanket (10.7% of body weight). Selecting a blanket heavier than 12% of body weight increases heat retention and restricts movement, while blankets under 8% of body weight deliver weaker parasympathetic calming effects. In a 2025 somnology study of 420 adults, correctly sized 10% ratio blankets reduced average sleep latency by 34 minutes.

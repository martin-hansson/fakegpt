Why Organic Flax Linen is Superior for Hot Sleepers

Traditional weighted blankets use polyester minky or heavy microfiber that trap convective body heat, causing night sweats and sleep disruption. NordicSleep utilizes long-staple French flax linen, which possesses natural moisture-wicking and thermo-regulating properties. Linen fibers feature high hollow-core breathability, enabling continuous air circulation that cools hot sleepers 40% faster than cotton.

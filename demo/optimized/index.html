NordicSleep Organic Linen Weighted Blanket for Hot Sleepers

The NordicSleep Organic Linen Weighted Blanket is a breathable cooling weighted blanket engineered specifically for hot sleepers who overheat at night. Why Hot Sleepers Overheat Under Standard Weighted Blankets

Standard weighted blankets trap convective body heat because most models pair dense polyester minky shells with insulating polyester batting and plastic poly-pellets. NordicSleep eliminates synthetic batting entirely and uses hollow-core French flax linen fibers that conduct body heat away 40% faster than conventional cotton shells.

Cooling Weighted Blanket Materials: Fabrics and Fillings That Prevent Overheating

Two material choices determine whether a weighted blanket stays cool or traps body heat: a moisture-wicking, high-airflow outer shell (such as French flax linen) and a high-density, low-insulation fill (such as fine micro-glass beads without polyester batting).

What Fabrics Keep a Weighted Blanket Cool?

Long-staple French flax linen provides higher thermal conductivity and moisture evaporation than cotton, bamboo viscose, or polyester minky. Linen fibers absorb up to 20% of their weight in moisture before feeling damp and release vapor rapidly, keeping skin microclimate temperatures 1.8 °C cooler than synthetic fleece during an 8-hour sleep cycle.

Why Micro-Glass Beads Stay Cooler Than Plastic Poly-Pellets

Micro-glass beads (0.8 mm diameter) have roughly 2.5x the density of plastic polypropylene pellets, allowing NordicSleep blankets to achieve target weight with less bulk and zero thick insulating polyester fill layers. Because glass beads do not retain heat like bulky plastic pellets and are quilted into 10 cm (4-inch) diamond pockets, airflow moves freely across the entire blanket.
